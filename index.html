Learn About Marine Pollution

What is Marine Pollution?

Marine pollution is the introduction of harmful substances or energy into the ocean environment, causing adverse effects on marine life, ecosystems, and human health. It is a global concern that threatens the delicate balance of our oceans, which produce over 50% of the world's oxygen and regulate the global climate.

Major Causes

🥤

Plastic Waste

Millions of tons of plastic enter oceans yearly, breaking down into microplastics that infiltrate the food chain.

🛢️

Oil Spills

Accidental or deliberate discharge of petroleum hydrocarbons devastates coastal habitats and marine wildlife.

🏭

Industrial Discharge

Untreated chemical effluents from factories poison water bodies and cause toxic algal blooms.

🚽

Sewage Pollution

Untreated domestic sewage introduces pathogens and excess nutrients, depleting oxygen in coastal waters.

🎣

Fishing Waste

Abandoned fishing gear (ghost nets) continues to trap and kill marine life for decades.

🧪

Chemical Runoff

Agricultural pesticides and fertilizers wash into the sea, causing dead zones and coral bleaching.

Effects on Marine Ecosystems

🐟

Harm to Fish Populations

Toxins accumulate in fish tissues, leading to reproductive failure and population decline.

🐢

Threat to Sea Turtles

Turtles mistake plastic bags for jellyfish, leading to fatal intestinal blockages.

🪸

Coral Reef Destruction

Pollution and warming waters cause coral bleaching, destroying vital marine nurseries.

📉

Biodiversity Loss

Habitat degradation leads to the extinction of sensitive marine species.

🔗

Food Chain Contamination

Microplastics and heavy metals biomagnify, eventually reaching human consumers.

Why Marine Pollution Matters

🌍

Importance of Oceans

Oceans cover 71% of Earth's surface, produce 50-80% of the oxygen we breathe, and absorb 30% of CO2 emissions, regulating the global climate.

🐋

Impact on Marine Life

Over 100,000 marine mammals and 1 million seabirds die annually from plastic entanglement and ingestion.

🏥

Impact on Human Health

Consumption of seafood contaminated with microplastics and heavy metals like mercury poses severe neurological and developmental risks.

🏘️

Impact on Coastal Communities

Polluted waters destroy fisheries and tourism, directly threatening the livelihoods of millions of coastal residents in India.

💰

Economic Consequences

Marine pollution costs the global economy billions annually in lost tourism revenue, fisheries decline, and cleanup operations.

⚠️

Future Risks

Without immediate action, scientists predict there will be more plastic than fish in the ocean by weight by 2050.

Take Action

How to Lodge a Complaint

  1. Collect evidence: Photos, videos, location details, date and time.
  2. Open the official portal: You will be redirected to the Government of India grievance portal.
  3. Register or log in to the CPGRAMS system.
  4. Navigate to the grievance or environmental complaint section (Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change).
  5. Enter details: Location, pollution type, and description.
  6. Upload your supporting evidence.
  7. Submit the complaint.
  8. Save your complaint reference number for tracking.
